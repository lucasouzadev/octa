# ADR-0003 — 3D in spot use only

Date: 2026-11-19 · Status: accepted (owner chose "pontual")

## Decision
3D (Threlte/Three.js) is used only for thumbnail objects: the fanned collection cards, the collection paper stack and the dead-link thumb. Playing video, controls and text are always 2D. Every 3D component ships with a CSS-3D fallback used for reduced motion, missing WebGL, low-end devices and Save-Data.

## Why
Embeds (YouTube/Instagram/TikTok iframes) inside WebGL are not possible and video plus a live 3D scene is heavy on phones. Spot 3D keeps the identity without hurting playback.

## Rejected
A fully navigable 3D library space (owner option "protagonista"): higher impact, heavier on mobile.
